It aims to reduce manual attendance work, prevent proxy attendance, and improve record management. The system also includes answer sheet scanning, attendance reports, and SMS or email notifications to parents or guardians.

02

IDntPrint

IDntPrint is a campus-based printing request system for students of the University of Northern Philippines. It aims to reduce long queues and waiting times by allowing students to upload documents, monitor real-time printing queues, and track their request status. It provides a faster and more organized printing experience.

  • Web dev
  • Database
  • Real-time queue management
